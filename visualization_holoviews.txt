# Holoviews: "Descriptive Graphics"

* When compared to bokeh or Matplotlib, Holoviews focuses on bundling 
  data with the appropriate metadata to support both analysis and 
  visualization, making your raw data and its visualization equally 
  accessible at all times:<br/>
  **instead of building plots programmatically, with direct calls to
  the plotting  library, we describe data with a small amount of crucial
  semantic information required to make it visualizable, then we
  specify additional metadata as needed to fine-tune the visualization**.

* Uses or Bokeh or Matplotlib as low-level plotting library.
